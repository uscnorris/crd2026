Poster Session

Share your research

Everyone is welcome to present — faculty, staff, trainees, and research centers, across every program.

Award prizes are for trainees. Anyone may submit and present a poster, but the judged competition and its three prizes are open only to students, postdoctoral fellows, and clinical fellows.

Registration and poster submission use the same form. Trainees entering the competition: please list your PI when you submit.

First time at a poster session? Here's what to expect

1
Walk the rows. Dozens of posters line Pappas Quad — browse in any order.
2
Talk to presenters. Say hi and ask for the "two-minute version" — totally normal to ask for.
3
Ask questions. "What surprised you?" or "What's next?" go a long way. No expertise required.
4
Use the app. Log the people you meet and request a follow-up connection.

How trainee posters are judged

Trainee entries are judged by Cancer Center faculty and senior fellows, using a standard rubric across every program — the same categories used at most major cancer research conferences.

1
Scientific merit. The significance of the question and the soundness of the approach.
2
Clarity of presentation. How well the poster and the presenter communicate the work.
3
Originality. What's novel about the approach or the findings.
4
Overall impression. Judges' holistic assessment of the poster and the conversation.
