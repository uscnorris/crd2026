The afternoon poster session is open to everyone at the Cancer Center: faculty, staff, trainees, and research centers, across every program.

Where posters are set up

Posters are grouped by research program around Pappas Quad. Your poster number tells you which side to look on — numbers run in order counterclockwise, starting on the Hoffman side.

Schematic only, not to scale. Board numbers are posted on the boards. Check-in is outside, at the staff table by the KAM steps.

Awards

Three prizes of $100

Trainees and staff who present a poster are automatically entered. Posters are judged by faculty alongside judges from the Norris Cancer Survivorship Advisory Council, who select three winners — one of whom also receives Survivors' Choice.

What judges look for

  • Scientific merit. The significance of the question and the soundness of the approach.
  • Clarity of presentation. How well the poster and the presenter communicate the work.
  • Originality. What is new about the approach or the findings.
  • Overall impression. The judges' holistic view of the poster and the conversation.

First time at a poster session?

1
Walk the rows. Dozens of posters line Pappas Quad. Browse in any order.
2
Talk to presenters. Say hi and ask for the two-minute version. It is a completely normal thing to ask for.
3
Ask questions. "What surprised you?" or "What's next?" go a long way. No expertise required.
4
Use the app. Browse the directory to see who's presenting and what they're working on.

Coffee Consult

New for 2026. One conversation across the bench-to-bedside divide, arranged for you.

Who it pairs
One research trainee (PhD student or postdoctoral fellow) with one clinical trainee (clinical fellow, resident, or clinical doctoral student).
What you talk about
The clinical reasoning behind the science: what a finding would mean at the bedside, and what questions the clinic actually needs answered.
How you get matched
Coffee Consult pairs people who opted in when they registered; sign-ups are now closed. If you opted in, open the Coffee Consult tab in the directory, find someone on the other side, and request a match by Monday, Oct 19. CRTEC arranges every match, so nobody is cold-contacting anyone.
If you opted in at registration, open the Coffee Consult tab in the directory, pick up to two people from the other group, and tap Request Coffee Consult. The first time, enter the email you registered with. You can request until Monday, Oct 19.
Not presenting a poster?
Come to the Coffee Consult table at the McKibben/Hoffman corner of Pappas Quad, 1:00–1:30 PM or 2:00–2:30 PM, to meet trainees from the other group in person.
What happens next
If you are matched, CRTEC emails you both with meeting guidance and a gift card, so you can meet for coffee or a beverage of your choice in the coming weeks.
