Good to know

Details & FAQ

Who can attend?

Everyone in the USC Norris Cancer Center community — faculty, postdocs, fellows, trainees, staff, and students. Registration is free.

Do I have to present a poster?

No. You're welcome to come for the talks, the panel, and the networking.

What are the poster requirements?

Posters must be no larger than 4 × 4 feet. Include your title and, if you're a trainee, your PI's name and program when you submit.

Is lunch provided?

Yes — lunch is served on Pappas Quad during the midday break.
