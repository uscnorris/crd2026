Why come

One day, the whole science of USC Norris

It's the one day a year the whole Cancer Center comes together in one place — come for the science, and leave with a few new collaborators.

See the whole landscape

Basic, translational, clinical, population, and community research, all under one roof for the day.

State of the science

Morning talks lead into this year's featured session on AI in cancer research and what it means for patients.

Real connections

A structured way to meet mentors, peers, clinicians, and patient advocates, with real follow-up afterward — not just small talk.

Showcase your work

Present a poster, get feedback from across disciplines, and compete for a scientific award.

Poster Session

Share your research

The afternoon poster session is open to trainees and researchers across every program. Three posters receive a scientific award.

Registration and poster submission use the same form. Trainees: please list your PI when you submit.

First time at a poster session? Here's what to expect

1
Walk the rows. Dozens of posters line Pappas Quad — browse in any order.
2
Talk to presenters. Researchers stand by their posters — say hi, and don't be afraid to ask for the two-minute version.
3
Ask questions. "What surprised you?" or "What's next?" go a long way, and you don't need to be an expert in the topic.
4
Use the app. Log the people you meet and request a follow-up connection.
Connect on the day

Meet people worth knowing

The directory is open any time — browse beforehand to see who's coming and plan who to visit, or use it live during the poster session to find people, log conversations, and request a short follow-up. CRTEC coordinates every match, so you're never cold-contacting anyone, and no one's cold-contacting you.

A note for faculty: Mentor Match only lists faculty who opt in when they register, and CRTEC books every request on your behalf — so it's a light, occasional commitment, not an open inbox.

Ready to connect? Browse the directory now to see who's presenting, or come back to it during the poster session to log who you meet.
Plan your visit

Getting there & good to know

A Mayer Auditorium (Keith Administration Bldg) — talks & featured session   B Pappas Quad — check-in, posters & lunch. Both on Zonal Ave, about a 2-minute walk apart. Open in Google Maps →

Can't make it in person?

Register as a virtual attendee and you can still join the talks and featured session by livestream — and get matched for a follow-up connection, just like in-person guests. The link goes live the morning of the event.

Continuing education credit

We are pursuing continuing education / CME credit for the day through the Keck School of Medicine CME office. Details and how to claim credit will be posted here and shared with registrants once confirmed.

Accessibility

The registration form asks whether you need any accommodations — seating, interpreting, materials, or anything else — and you're welcome to email us any time as well.

Food & dietary needs

Lunch is provided on Pappas Quad, and the registration form asks about dietary restrictions so we can plan for everyone.
