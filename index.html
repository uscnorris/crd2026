Why come

Poster Session

Share your research

The afternoon poster session is open to trainees and researchers across every program. Three posters receive a scientific award.

Registration and poster submission use the same form. Trainees: please list your PI when you submit.

First time at a poster session? Here's what to expect

1
Walk the rows. Dozens of printed posters line Pappas Quad. Browse whatever catches your eye — there's no set order.
2
Talk to presenters. Researchers stand by their posters. Say hi and ask them to walk you through it — a "two-minute version" is a totally normal thing to ask for.
3
Ask questions. "What surprised you?" or "What's next?" go a long way. You don't need to be an expert in the topic.
4
Use the app. Log the people you meet and request a follow-up connection. It's casual, come and go as you like.
Connect on the day

Meet people worth knowing

During the poster session you can use our directory to find people, log the conversations you have, and request a short follow-up. Each connection is a track that builds our training community. CRTEC coordinates every match — you won't be cold-contacting anyone, and no one will be cold-contacting you.

A note for faculty: Mentor Match only lists faculty who opt in when they register, and every request is booked through CRTEC — so it's a light, opt-in commitment, not an open inbox.

Ready to connect? Open the directory to browse posters and people, then log who you meet.
Plan your visit

Getting there & good to know

Schematic, not to scale. The day runs between Mayer Auditorium (morning talks) and Pappas Quad (poster session & lunch), on the Health Sciences Campus.

Can't make it in person?

You can still take part. Register as a virtual attendee to join the talks and featured session by livestream. Virtual registrants can also submit a short bio on the form to be matched for a follow-up connection, so you don't miss the networking. Livestream link goes to registered attendees before the event.

Continuing education credit

We are pursuing continuing education / CME credit for the day through the Keck School of Medicine CME office. Details and how to claim credit will be posted here and shared with registrants once confirmed.

Accessibility

We want everyone to take part fully. The registration form asks whether you need any accommodations (seating, interpreting, materials, or anything else). You can also email us any time and we'll make arrangements.

Food & dietary needs

Lunch is provided on Pappas Quad. The registration form asks about dietary restrictions (vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, allergies, and more) so we can plan options for everyone.
